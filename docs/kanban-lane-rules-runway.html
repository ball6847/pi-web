Kanban lane rules runway auto prompt

One card, six lanes. The rules are injected before the work, not after it.

Why a runway, not just the current lane?

Plan's rule must arrive while Todo work is still being planned. A rule injected only after the card enters Plan is too late: the work that creates that lane is already finished.

Why not repeat it every turn?

The injected custom message carries a signature: position > laneId#hash(text) > …. Same signature means no new message. Entry, edit, attach and compaction change it; a plain turn does not.

Scope and budget

Only the session's own card; subagents get none. Text is capped at 2,000 chars per lane and 4,000 chars total. Lanes that do not fit are named, not silently dropped.
